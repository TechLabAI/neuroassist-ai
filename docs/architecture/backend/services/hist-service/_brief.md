> Module: **HIST — Histórico e Auditoria** · Service: `hist-service` · Package: `com.neuroassist`
> Project: NeuroAssist AI · Architecture: independent microservices (ADR-002 shared) · Messaging: disabled
> Generated by `/be-architect-vision neuroassist-ai HIST` · 2026-08-16

## 1. Canonical name and prefix
- **Prefix:** `HIST`
- **Canonical name:** `hist`
- **Service:** `hist-service` (Java 21 / Spring Boot backend module)

## 2. Problem the module solves
Every clinically or legally meaningful thing that happens to a patient's help requests — an event was
raised, an alarm was delivered, a caregiver confirmed it was `real` or dismissed it as `falso_positivo`,
a command's sensitivity was edited, a caregiver's access changed — must be provable **after the fact** and
**impossible to rewrite**. The operational services (EVNT, PAC, ACS, EDGE) keep *current* state and are
free to mutate it; none of them is a trustworthy witness of *what happened and when*. HIST is that witness:
a single, **append-only, immutable audit ledger** that persists audit-worthy facts as they occur and lets a
caregiver or coordinator later reconstruct the exact timeline of a patient's care — for trust, for
accountability, and for LGPD-grade traceability of who did what.

## 3. Personas
- **Primary — Jamilly Amaral (family caregiver):** wants to look back and see, for her patient, the honest
  sequence of alarms and confirmations ("was yesterday's 3am alarm real or a false positive, and who
  confirmed it?"). She reads history; she never edits it.
- **Secondary — Danilo Amaral (technical conductor / clinic coordinator):** during onboarding and audits
  needs a defensible trail — who changed a command's sensitivity, who was granted access to a patient,
  which emergencies were missed or acknowledged — to tune the system and satisfy accountability.
- **Indirect — Márcio Amaral (patient) / data-protection officer:** the patient is the subject whose care
  timeline is being recorded; the DPO relies on the ledger as the LGPD record of processing activities and
  access to patient data. Neither operates the service directly.

## 4. MVP scope (in)
- **Audit fact ingestion**: accept an audit record (`AuditFact`) from any producing service over REST (via
  GWY), **idempotent on a producer-supplied `factId`**, and persist it write-once into the tenant ledger.
- **Immutable, append-only storage**: records are never updated or deleted through the API; a "correction"
  is a new fact that references the prior one, never an in-place edit (ADR-004).
- **Tamper-evident chaining**: each record carries a hash chained to the previous record in its
  (tenant, subject) stream, so any silent alteration or gap is detectable on read (ADR-006).
- **History & audit query**: read APIs to retrieve the ordered timeline for a patient/subject and to filter
  by fact type, actor and time window — the data source DASH renders as "Histórico".
- **Actor & subject attribution**: every fact records *who* (actor caregiver ref), *what* (fact type +
  payload snapshot), *which subject* (patient/command/caregiver ref) and *when* (occurred-at + recorded-at),
  strictly tenant-scoped.

## 5. Out of scope (MVP)
- Being the system of record for operational state — EVNT owns the `Evento`, PAC the `Comando`, ACS the
  `Cuidador`. HIST **mirrors audit facts**; it never replaces their live tables.
- Emitting or dispatching notifications/alarms (EVNT) and rendering the history UI (DASH) — HIST serves
  data; DASH renders it.
- Computer vision, gesture detection, device pairing/heartbeat (GEST/EDGE), and any video/image/facial
  landmark data — HIST stores only structured business facts, never biometric payloads.
- Analytics, aggregation dashboards, ML on the trail, and long-term cold archival/warehousing — MVP keeps
  the ledger queryable and durable for the pilot lifetime; no archival tiering.
- Any asynchronous broker / event bus (messaging disabled MVP) — producers call HIST synchronously over REST.
- Cross-tenant reporting or a global audit console — every query is scoped to a single `empresa`.

## 6. Mandatory external integrations
- **EVNT** (`evnt-service`) — primary producer: posts audit facts for event lifecycle transitions
  (event raised, notified, confirmed `real` / `falso_positivo`, resolved).
- **PAC** (`pac-service`) — producer: command calibration/sensitivity edits and command retire/version facts.
- **ACS** (`acs-service`) — producer: caregiver access grants/revocations and caregiver↔patient linkage
  changes; also the **authority resolved by reference** for actor identity.
- **EDGE** (`edge-service`) — producer: device pairing / credential lifecycle facts (audit-worthy).
- **DASH** (`dash-service`) — consumer: reads the history/audit query APIs to render "Histórico".
- **GWY / REG** — all producer→HIST and DASH→HIST calls are REST routed via the Gateway and discovered via
  Eureka (independent microservices, ADR-002 shared).

## 7. Hub / TechLab AI dependencies & inter-module contracts
- **TechLab AI services:** none. `ai.hasAi = false`; HIST is deterministic persistence and retrieval, no
  inference.
- **Messaging:** disabled (`stack.messaging.enabled = false`) → all audit-fact ingestion is **synchronous
  REST** from producers to hist-service (routed via GWY, discovered via REG). No event bus in the MVP; the
  broker-based fan-out of audit facts is explicitly deferred (ADR-005).
- **Consumes (by reference, snapshotted):** actor identity (ACS `Cuidador`), subject refs (patient/command
  ids from PAC/EVNT). **Produces/owns:** the immutable `AuditRecord` ledger (read by DASH). HIST is a
  **sink**, not a source of operational truth.

## 8. Domain anchor event
- **"Fato auditado" (audit fact recorded)** — the moment an audit-worthy action becomes a write-once,
  hash-chained, tenant-scoped record in the ledger, permanently attributable and impossible to alter. HIST
  owns each record's lifecycle, which is exactly one transition: `recorded` (terminal — there is no update
  or delete).
